# 💫 About Me:
🚀A hobbyist Robotics dev! I am a Senior year CS Student, tech in Digital Electronics, and (slowly) learning robotics on my own! Very enthusiastic about simulation, control algorithms and many real-time, data-driven algorithms!<br>🔭 I'm currently working on a custom ROS-like framework in Rust built on Zenoh for my own projects! Also really like parallel compute in robotics, and currently tinkering with MPPI.<br>👯 I'd love to collab on interesting projects with data insight and iterative improvement processes!<br>🤝I'd love to collaborate developing efficient ways for communication and control, and better uses of compute power!<br>🌱I am learning the mathematical basis of robotics (and most of programming, for that matter). Also working on my teamwork skills, and general abilities!<br>💬 You can ask me about my projects if you're interested, I'd love to chat about robotics in general, and just someone to learn from and work with!<br>⚡Fun Fact: I am very passionate about listening to music, and tinkering with electronics!
